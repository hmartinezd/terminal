# ADR-013: Sales export share handoff

Sales exports use the same immutable `PreparedSalesExport` JSON and exact revision set for both document saving and Android sharing. Sharing writes that JSON to an app-controlled cache file, exposes only its `content://` URI through `FileProvider`, and launches the standard `ACTION_SEND` chooser with temporary read permission.

A share is considered exported only after the cache write, content-URI creation, and successful chooser launch. “Shared” therefore means handed to Android's share mechanism, not confirmed recipient delivery or processing. Failures before chooser launch do not mark revisions exported. Exact prepared revisions are marked after handoff, preserving concurrent lifecycle changes and duplicate-safe retry behavior.

Share files older than 24 hours are removed when a later share is prepared. They are not deleted immediately because receiving applications may read them after chooser launch.
