# ADR-004: Sales Terminal normally treats imported menu/prices as read-only

## Status
Accepted

## Context
To maintain consistency and financial integrity, terminals should not modify the authoritative pricing received in a MenuPackage.

## Decision
Sales Terminal treats the imported menu and its prices as read-only for its operational lifecycle. 

## Consequences
- Simplifies terminal logic.
- Ensures reports generated on the terminal match the expected commercial configuration.
