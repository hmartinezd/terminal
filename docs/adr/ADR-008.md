# ADR-008: Commercial revision, consumption revision, and publication revision are independent concepts

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Different aspects of the menu (prices, recipes, whole package) change at different rates and for different reasons.

## Decision
We will track three separate revision dimensions:
- `publicationRevision`: Version of the whole `MenuPackage`.
- `commercialRevision`: Changes to prices, names, categories, etc.
- `consumptionRevision`: Changes to inventory-consumption meaning (recipes).

## Consequences
- Sales Terminal can track what inventory meaning to report without understanding the recipes.
- Fine-grained tracking of changes.
